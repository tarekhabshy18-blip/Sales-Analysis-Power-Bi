           
Sales Analysis Dashboard — Business Insights
1. Regional Sales Performance
Finding: Cairo recorded the highest total net sales among all regions.
Business Interpretation: Cairo is the leading region in terms of sales revenue. Understanding the factors behind its performance may help the business identify opportunities to improve sales in other regions.
Recommendation: Analyze order volume, average order value, product preferences, and discount rates across regions to identify the main drivers of Cairo's performance.
2. Product Category Performance
Finding: Electronics generated the highest total sales among all product categories.
Business Interpretation: Electronics is the leading product category in terms of sales revenue and may represent an important area of the business's sales activity.
Recommendation: Evaluate product-level sales, profit margins, inventory availability, and discount levels within the Electronics category before making decisions about stock allocation or promotional campaigns.
3. Payment Method Analysis
Finding: Card payments accounted for the highest total sales value among the available payment methods.
Business Interpretation: Card payments are associated with the largest share of sales revenue in this dataset. This may indicate that card transactions are important to the business's sales activity.
Recommendation: Compare the number of transactions, average order value, transaction fees, and payment success rates across payment methods to understand customer payment behavior and its financial implications.
4. Overall Business Recommendations
•	Regional Strategy: Investigate the factors driving Cairo's sales performance and assess opportunities in lower-performing regions.
•	Product Strategy: Monitor Electronics sales, profitability, and inventory levels to support effective product planning.
•	Payment Strategy: Evaluate both transaction volume and revenue by payment method before prioritizing payment options.
•	Performance Monitoring: Track sales, order volume, average order value, and discount rates regularly to identify changes in business performance.
